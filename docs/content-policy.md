## Evidence labels

- **Public source:** source or reproduction evidence is linked.
- **Sanitized note:** the work is private; only claim-safe engineering scope is described.
- **Private work:** referenced without publishing implementation detail.
- **Hold:** not ready for public positioning.

## Writing rules

- Prefer concrete system boundaries, decisions, tests, and limitations to adjectives.
- Describe the implementation context and evidence behind each material claim.
- Do not claim sole ownership when work was collaborative.
- Do not claim legal compliance from technical controls.
- Do not claim business impact without a verified measurement.
- Mark simulations and proxies where a reader could mistake them for real-domain validation.
- Draft generic advice until it is tied to an inspectable implementation or evidence trail.
