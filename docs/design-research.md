# Design research

Research date: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

This study asks how VillaForTech can feel like the personal platform of a technically ambitious future founder while remaining precise, bilingual, fast, and useful to founders, engineering leaders, collaborators, potential clients, and event organizers.

The references are not templates. Each was reviewed for hierarchy, typography, rhythm, navigation, storytelling, proof, calls to action, motion, mobile behavior, recognizability, and failure modes.

## Reference review

### 1. Daniel Priestley

URL: <https://danielpriestley.com/>

- Hierarchy and rhythm: the commercial promise arrives before the biography, followed by proof, offers, speaking, media, and books. Large headings alternate with compact evidence blocks.
- Typography and navigation: bold display typography and a direct navigation system support an overtly commercial voice.
- Storytelling and trust: named companies, books, speaking topics, media appearances, and audience figures establish authority quickly.
- Calls to action: multiple routes serve business owners, readers, and event organizers without hiding the main offer.
- Motion and mobile: short entrance sequences add energy, but repeated promotional modules make the narrow-screen journey long.
- Recognizable quality: one repeated positioning phrase and a full ecosystem of authored products make the site unmistakably his.
- Do not copy: high-pressure funnel language, repeated slogans, oversized proof walls, or conversion density that would overpower Roberto's technical evidence.

### 2. Chip Huyen

URL: <https://huyenchip.com/>

- Typography and navigation: simple typography and minimal navigation let the evidence carry the page.
- Storytelling and trust: her path, named roles, books, teaching, public tools, and research links create both humanity and authority.
- Calls to action: collaboration is invited naturally rather than through a sales funnel.

## Motion and accessibility references

- [W3C Technique C39](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C39.html): place nonessential motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the reduced experience should suppress motion, not merely accelerate it.
- [Astro view transitions](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/view-transitions/): native transitions and route announcements exist, but client routing should not be added solely for decoration.
- [web.dev animation performance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-and-performance): prefer opacity and transforms, preserve layout, and avoid blanket `will-change`.
- [Emil Kowalski, Great Animations](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/great-animations): interaction feedback should be fast, interruptible, and explain a state change.

## Five principles to adopt

1. Make evidence traceable. Important claims should lead to a case, source, architecture artifact, or explicitly bounded explanation.
2. Organize around visitor intent. Founders, engineering leaders, collaborators, and speaking organizers should each recognize a useful next path.
3. Let artifacts carry the reputation. Boundaries, decisions, tradeoffs, and evidence matter more than adjective-heavy positioning.
4. Build the visual language from the work. Signals, boundaries, routes, state changes, provenance, and human checkpoints are VillaForTech's native vocabulary.
5. Design the static mobile document first. Motion and hover may enrich it, but never provide required meaning.

## Five generic patterns to reject

1. WebGL spectacle, audio, scroll hijacking, parallax, cursor followers, and preloaders.
2. Vanity counters, unverifiable metrics, vague logo walls, and testimonial placeholders.
3. Technology-logo soup, fake terminals, glowing AI imagery, glass panels, and decorative gradients.
4. Empty newsletters, blogs, digital gardens, or taxonomies added to imitate established writers.
5. Repeated funnel copy, artificial urgency, word-by-word reveals, and motion applied to every object.

## Three possible high-level improvements

### A. Turn projects into compact evidence records

Expose the system boundary, the key decision, and the available evidence before a visitor opens a case study. Lead with the only public-source project and distinguish inspectable evidence from sanitized work without diminishing either.

### B. Build a recognizable system-boundary grammar

Use a disciplined family of lines, ports, nodes, state changes, and human checkpoints across the hero and cases. Animate only meaningful signal movement and preserve the complete static state.

### C. Route professional audiences explicitly

After the opening proof, give founders and engineering leaders distinct reasons to continue. Both paths can converge on selected work and contact while consulting, startup opportunities, collaboration, and speaking remain visible.

## Recommended direction

**Editorial systems in motion.**

Keep the paper-and-ink identity, serif scale, monospace evidence labels, hard rules, and human portrait. Strengthen it with a single VillaForTech-specific signal-and-boundary motif, earlier public proof, compact visitor routing, more visual case-study structures, contextual endings, and restrained motion.

The motion vocabulary is deliberately small:

- Hero entrance: at most four semantic groups, 360ms, 45ms stagger, 8px travel.
- Section reveal: one run, 8px to 12px travel, no more than three staggered siblings.
- Controls: 140ms to 200ms transform, color, border, or underline feedback with hover and focus parity.
- Diagram signal: one finite sequence, 520ms maximum per state, then stop.
- Reduced motion: final states render immediately with no hidden information and no movement.

This direction is original because its form comes from Roberto's actual engineering practice, not from an imported visual trend.
