## The question

How can recording personal finances take less effort while keeping the person in control of what gets saved?

Panorama brings together expenses, income, budgets, subscriptions, and plans. The product challenge is to make capture simple while keeping the result understandable: what was interpreted, what still needs confirmation, and how the summary will change.

## From a sentence to an entry

The flow separates four moments:

1. **Capture.** Write, speak, or provide a receipt.
2. **Interpret.** Propose structured information from the input.
3. **Review.** Check the amount, type, and other details before confirming.
4. **Record.** Save the decision and update the system’s calculations.

The illustration uses a fictional example: “I spent $8 on lunch.” The proposed expense stays outside the summary until it has been reviewed and confirmed. This is an editorial representation of the flow, not a screenshot of an account or an executable session.

## Where AI stops

Interpretation proposes; the financial domain calculates. The system represents amounts as integer cents and applies explicit rules to distinguish recorded money, consumption, and forecasts.

This separation keeps a model response from being treated as a confirmed operation on its own. It also makes calculations and state changes testable against known cases.

The summary distinguishes money already spent from planned spending. Expected income is not yet received money, and an amount declared by a person is not a bank-verified balance.

## Building the whole journey

The client uses React Native and Expo to share work across web and mobile. The API uses TypeScript and Fastify, with PostgreSQL persistence and an interpretation adapter through Amazon Bedrock.

The work includes interface design, client–server contracts, review states, persistence, and attempt recovery. Even a correct interpretation still needs an experience that helps the person understand, correct, and save it consistently.

The version described here does not connect to banks or import email. It organizes information provided by the person; it does not replace bank verification or provide investment recommendations.

## What I’m learning

Building with AI means paying as much attention to the model’s boundaries as to the rest of the product. In Panorama, the decisive step lies between a proposal and a confirmed entry: preserving the person’s intent, showing what will happen, and keeping calculations open to inspection.
